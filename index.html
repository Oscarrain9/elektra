Our Services

Residential Electrical Installations

We offer professional installation of various home appliances such as stoves, ovens, refrigerators, air conditioning units, and more. Our electricians will ensure that your appliances are safely connected and compliant with local codes.

Outdoor and Landscape Lighting

We provide expert installation of outdoor lighting systems that enhance your property's beauty and functionality. From pathway lights to garden lighting and security lights, we can help make your outdoor spaces safer and more attractive.

Electric Vehicle Charging Stations

With the growing popularity of electric vehicles, we specialize in the installation of EV charging stations for residential and commercial use. Our team ensures that your charging station is installed safely and efficiently.

Electrical Wiring and System Upgrades

If your electrical system is outdated or you're planning renovations, we offer electrical rewiring and system upgrades. This includes circuit breaker installations, electrical panel upgrades, and more to ensure the safety and reliability of your electrical system.

Smart Home Electrical Systems

We offer installation of smart home devices like thermostats, automated lighting systems, security cameras, and voice-activated assistants like Amazon Alexa or Google Home. Our team integrates these systems seamlessly into your home's electrical infrastructure.
